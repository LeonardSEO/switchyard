# Switchyard contributors

Switchyard is strengthened by people who test its assumptions, report concrete
failures, review routing behavior, and contribute focused fixes.

## Early contributor — Routing & Reliability

### [Tai Benvenuti (@taibenvenuti)](https://github.com/taibenvenuti)

Tai has provided detailed technical reviews of Switchyard's classification,
Pi/OMP adapter contracts, admission handling, and outcome-learning model. His
reports included reproducible evidence and practical remediation ideas that led
directly to stronger routing behavior in releases 0.3.5 and 0.3.6.

Tai is especially invited to review routing and reliability changes, propose
improvements, and submit focused pull requests.

## Contributing

Thoughtful issues, reviews, tests, documentation, and focused pull requests are
all valuable contributions. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) to get
started.
